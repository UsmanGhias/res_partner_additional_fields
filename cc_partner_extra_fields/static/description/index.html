Standardize Enterprise B2B Contact Profiles

Out of the box, Odoo provides standard contact details such as address, phone, and tax ID. However, enterprise operations, GCC businesses, and B2B traders require critical statutory and operational identifiers: Commercial Registration (CR) numbers, organization classifications, verification status, and default freight methods.

This module introduces structured fields directly onto res.partner, neatly organized under a dedicated Commercial & Compliance tab. All new fields integrate with Odoo list views, search filters, and group-by aggregations.

Module Capabilities

Commercial Registration (CR) & Codes

Maintain official trade license numbers, CR identifiers, and custom partner account codes. Fully searchable via the standard contacts search bar.

Organization & Entity Classification

Categorize entities into Corporate / Private Enterprise, Government, Semi-Government, NGO, Individual, or Foreign Company, alongside classification as Customer, Vendor, Contractor, or Partner.

Compliance & Onboarding Status

Monitor contact vetting state with visual badges on tree and form views: Active & Approved, Under Review, Suspended, or Blocked.

Logistics & Payment Preferences

Define default logistics arrangements (Air, Sea, Road, Express Courier, Pickup) and preferred payment channels (Bank Wire, Corporate Check, Card, Cash).
